# Release notes

## Build 7 — public source update, 9 October 2026

- Unlock in person with the PIN, or connect a trusted helper’s iPhone once and
  redeem their signed link for 15 minutes, one hour or until local midnight.
- Helper-only use needs no restrictions setup. Single-use links have a ten-minute
  redemption window; removal and re-pairing replace the relevant credentials.
- Persisted replay/clock protection, authoritative Keychain reads, visible
  navigation outcomes and successful-lease attribution share the existing timer.
- Public native/UI tests and build-6 migration fixtures reproduce without private
  history. README documents independent signing and the browser fallback.
- Safe example images and their asset manifest are available for reuse.

Charlie reports installing and successfully using unlock links on his and Bene’s
phones on 8 October 2026. The exact installed revision is unknown; unobserved
historical phone checks remain unobserved. Public unsigned checks do not prove
signed Keychain persistence, physical shielding or message delivery.
